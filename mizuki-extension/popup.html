Privacy first

Network access is off until you allow a server. Manual chat sends your message, recent chat history, personality, language and local time to that server. Its operator may use another AI provider; this extension cannot verify their retention policy.

Page reactions send the title, hostname and first headline of non-excluded sites, plus personality, language and local time. They appear inside the website and may be read by it. Private chat history is never included. Incognito reactions are disabled.

Hostnames only, separated by spaces or lines. Subdomains are included.

Settings apply immediately. Click or keyboard-focus the sprite to open chat; drag to move. On browser-protected pages, use the toolbar button instead.
